# Cover – Pro und Contra – KI- und IT-News

Nano Banana Pro (Leonardo.ai), **1:1**, Medium. Apple/Spotify: quadratisch, ideal 3000 × 3000 px, mindestens 1400 × 1400.
Idee: Die Namen sind das Konzept – Sommer = Sonne/warm/Aufbruch, Mondschein = Mond/kühl/Schattenseite.
Die Figuren sind frei erfunden (nicht den Stimmgebern Thorsten-Voice / Stefan Schmelz nachgebildet).

## Prompt

```
Square podcast cover art, 1:1, bold and graphic, readable even as a tiny thumbnail.

The image is split diagonally from the top right to the bottom left into day and night.
Left half: warm summer daylight, golden-orange sky with a stylised sun.
Right half: cool deep-blue night with a large crescent moon and a few stars.
Where the halves meet, a thin glowing line made of circuit-board traces.

Two fictional male podcast hosts face each other across the split, each in front of a
studio microphone, captured mid-debate. Semi-realistic editorial illustration style.

On the sunny left, "Herr Sommer": early 40s, open friendly face, animated gesture,
light shirt with rolled-up sleeves, a smartwatch, and a small smart-home gadget blinking
beside him. Warm rim light.

On the night side, "Herr Mondschein": late 50s, grey temples, reading glasses, dark
cardigan, arms crossed, one eyebrow raised in dry, ironic scepticism. Cool silver
moonlight.

Typography at the top, large, bold, clean sans-serif:
"PRO UND CONTRA"
Smaller line directly below: "KI- und IT-News"
Add a small "+" on the sun side and a small "−" on the moon side.

Limited palette: warm amber/orange against midnight blue and silver, high contrast.
Minimal background, no clutter, no extra text, no logos, no watermark.
```
